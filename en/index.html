Privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026

CoPill is an app to remember your medications and confirm each dose with the help of a caregiver. To do that it keeps information about your health, so we handle it with care: we only keep what is needed, we don't sell it, we don't show ads and we don't use analytics or tracking tools.

CoPill doesn't replace the advice of your doctor or pharmacist. It helps you remember and confirm your doses; if you have any question about your treatment, ask a health professional.

Who is responsible

Juan Bolaños is responsible for your data. For any question or request, write to juanmanu261@gmail.com.

What we keep and why

DataWhy
Name, email and password (stored as a hash, never in plain text)Create your account and sign you in.
Role (patient or caregiver) and time zoneShow you the right view and schedule your reminders at your time.
Medications, doses, schedules and alarmsRemind you of each dose.
Dose history and verification codesConfirm each dose with your caregiver and work out your adherence.
Link with your caregiver or your patientsLet your caregiver see your doses and send you reminders.
Notification preferencesRespect how you want to be notified.
Device identifier, platform and push notification tokenSend you reminders and keep your devices in sync.

Medications and doses are health data. We only use them to provide the service, with the consent you give when you sign up. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.

Who sees your data

  • You, on all your devices.
  • The caregiver you link sees your name, medications, schedules and the status of your doses. They don't see your verification codes or your preferences. You can unlink them at any time from Profile.
  • No one else. We don't sell or share your data with advertisers or other companies.

Services we use

  • Server and database: your data is stored with a cloud infrastructure provider, encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) and Apple Push Notification service: they deliver push notifications. Messages only carry identifiers and the kind of alert, never medication names or health data; the app writes the text on your phone.

On your phone

The app keeps a copy of your data to work offline. Your session is stored encrypted in the system's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android). When you log out or delete your account, the app deletes that copy.

How long we keep it

DataPeriod
Your account and everything in itAs long as the account exists. Deleting it erases everything at once and for good.
Records of deleted items (to sync your other devices)90 days.
Reminders and alerts already sent30 days after sending.

The server's technical logs contain no personal data, tokens or request contents.

How to delete your account

From the app

Open Profile → Account details → Delete account, type your password and confirm. Your account and all its data (profile, medications, alarms, dose history, care links, devices and sessions) are erased at once and for good.

Without the app

Write to juanmanu261@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my CoPill account” from your account's email. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Your rights

You can see and correct your data in the app, ask us for a copy, object to its use or delete it at any time. Write to the email above and we'll answer within 30 days.

Security

All communication is encrypted. Passwords are stored with a slow hash (PBKDF2), sessions expire and are renewed, and every request checks that you only reach your own data or that of the patients you supervise.

Children

CoPill is not directed to children under 13. If a child needs the app, a parent or guardian should create and manage the account.

Changes to this policy

If we change it, we'll update the date above and, if the change is important, let you know in the app.
